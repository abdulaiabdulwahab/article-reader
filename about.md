# About Ink & Insight

Ink & Insight is a simple home for articles about ideas, practical learning, daily life, and the digital world. It is designed to keep the reading experience comfortable and uncluttered.

The content included with this starter is demonstration material. Replace it with your own articles and editorial information before you launch your publication.

## Our approach

We aim for helpful writing, clear sources where needed, and a clean experience that works on both phones and computers.
